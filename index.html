About CoSAIL

CoSAIL is a research laboratory in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad, led by Dr. Ankit Gupta. We work at the intersection of machine learning, stochastic dynamical systems, probability theory, and scientific computing.

Stochastic models are essential across biology, physics, quantum systems, and finance, but their enormous state spaces make probabilities and statistics expensive to compute. Machine learning offers powerful tools, yet black-box approaches often ignore the underlying mathematical structure.

Our premise is that machine learning and mathematical modelling are complementary. By building the structure of stochastic systems into learning algorithms, we aim for methods that are more efficient, interpretable, and reliable, and that generalise better.

Our focus

Scalable, structure-aware machine learning for high-dimensional stochastic systems, including those driven by time-dependent inputs and feedback policies, with applications that help uncover mechanisms and new scientific insights.

Research

Our programme is built around a common computational core, CoSAIL-Markov, which supports six application directions.

CORE FRAMEWORK

CoSAIL-Markov

A GPU-accelerated computational framework for learning and analysing stochastic Markov dynamics. It is the methodological foundation of the laboratory.

  • Massively parallel GPU simulation
  • Learning probability evolution (the Master Equation)
  • Stochastic Koopman operators
  • Low-dimensional spectral learning
  • Parametric surrogates across inputs and parameters
  • Controlled dynamics with external inputs and feedback
SynBio

CoSAIL-SynBio

Spatial synthetic feedback control and robust regulation in living systems.

Phylo

CoSAIL-Phylo

Bayesian phylodynamic inference and pathogen genomic surveillance.

PDE

CoSAIL-PDE

Mesh-free PDE operator learning from microscopic Markov simulations.

Quantum

CoSAIL-Quantum

Reduced open quantum dynamics from trajectories and resolvent learning.

Finance

CoSAIL-Finance

Operator learning for derivatives pricing and portfolio optimisation.

GenAI

CoSAIL-GenAI

Structure-aware score estimation and diffusion-based generative modelling.
