Principal Investigator

Dr. Ankit Gupta

Associate Professor, Department of Artificial Intelligence, IIT Hyderabad · Head of CoSAIL

I am an applied mathematician working at the interface of probability theory, stochastic processes and machine learning. I develop computational, analytical and statistical methods for stochastic dynamical systems, with applications in biology, and increasingly in physics, finance and generative AI. Before joining IIT Hyderabad in 2026, I spent over fourteen years at the Department of Biosystems Science and Engineering, ETH Zurich, most recently as Permanent Senior Scientist.

Research

At CoSAIL, my group develops mathematically principled machine learning for stochastic systems. The programme is built around CoSAIL-Markov, a GPU-accelerated framework for learning and analysing Markov dynamics through the master equation, stochastic Koopman operators and low-dimensional spectral structure, with applications in synthetic biology, phylodynamics, PDEs, open quantum systems, finance and generative AI.

CoSAIL research programme

Looking at life through a mathematical lens

Much of this programme grows out of a decade of work on stochastic reaction networks, summarised below.

Earlier work, at ETH Zurich and before

Biomolecular control theory

Design and characterisation of intracellular circuits that achieve robust perfect adaptation (cybergenetics).

Master equation solvers

Projection-based, simulation-based and deep-learning methods (DeepCME) for high-dimensional chemical master equations.

Stochastic filtering

Particle filters and projection methods for inferring hidden states from observed single-cell trajectories.

Sensitivity analysis

Efficient, unbiased Monte Carlo estimators of parameter sensitivities for stochastic models.

Stability analysis

Computationally verifiable conditions for ergodicity of Markov chain models of reaction networks.

Frequency-domain analysis

Simulation-based estimation of the frequency spectrum of stochastic single-cell trajectories.

Noise in systems biology

How intracellular randomness shapes population-level behaviour, such as entrainment and signal amplification.

Phylodynamics

Bayesian inference of transmission trees and epidemiological parameters from pathogen phylogenies.

Adaptive dynamics & measure-valued processes

Limit theorems for evolutionary models, and Fleming–Viot processes arising in models of cell polarity.
